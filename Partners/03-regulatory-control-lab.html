Regulatory control in practice

Regulatory Control Lab works with regulated organisations that need to make operational resilience visible across technology, risk, procurement, and executive governance.

ICT risk management

Translate policy into ownership, controls, review cycles, and working evidence. Teams gain a practical view of important services and the technology that supports them.

Third-party oversight

Structure supplier information, criticality decisions, contracts, dependencies, and review actions so external technology risk can be managed continuously.

Incident readiness

Connect response procedures, communication plans, recovery priorities, and lessons learned. Every exercise produces actions that can be tracked to completion.

Management reporting

Present concise evidence to leadership and auditors, with a clear line from important business services to risks, controls, tests, findings, and remediation.
