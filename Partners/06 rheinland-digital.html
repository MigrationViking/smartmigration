Vom Assessment bis zum Betrieb

  1. Assessment with context
    Workshops with records owners and service desk staff, connected to the technical inventory — so the plan reflects how information is actually used.
  2. Test migration
    Detailed exception reports, cutover planning, and a documented ownership model for the destination.
  3. Adoption
    Department-level guidance so the new structure survives contact with everyday work.
  4. Operations after go-live
    Managed support, scheduled reporting reviews, and small improvement cycles.

Sprachen: Deutsch und Englisch, in Workshops wie in der Dokumentation.
